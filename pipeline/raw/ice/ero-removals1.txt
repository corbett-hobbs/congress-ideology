NOTE: If an alien was identified by more than one priority they were defaulted to the highest priority based on the following hierarchy (Ordered Highest to Lowest): Convicted Criminal, Immigration Fugitives, Immigration Repeat Violators, Removable Aliens.

Convicted Criminals - Those that are convicted of any crime prior to their departure.
Immigration Fugitives - Latest Processing Disposition Code before departure is Bag and Baggage (B) OR Case Category at time of Latest Arrest was Fugitive (8E, 8I, 5B).
Repeat Immigration Violators - Anyone with prior departure or prior voluntary return.
Recent Border Entrants - Removals initiated by CBP at or near Border. Based on Latest Program Code before departure that is Border Patrol (BP) or Inspections (ISA, ISL, ISS, ISP), Latest Apprehension Method Code before departure that is Inspections (ISP) or Patrol
Border (PB), Case Category of 8F, 8G or 8H associated with the Removal case, or an Entry Date within three years of Departed Date.

Other Removable Aliens - This category includes those aliens who entered unlawfully or entered lawfully and violated conditions of admission; aliens who may also fall into the above priorities but cannot be verified in the data available to ICE (i.e. criminals with no
recorded conviction).




Data used to report on ICE statistics are obtained through the ICE Integrated Decision Support (IIDS) system data warehouse. The records in this system are refreshed on a daily basis, and typically reflect any updates to an alien’s case as new information becomes available
to ICE. Data obtained from this system will reflect the status of these records as of the date the data is retrieved. Any records that have yet to be administratively updated in the IIDS warehouse will not be reflected in the data reported. The information retrieved from the
IIDS warehouse changes on a daily basis, due to the dynamic nature of the process of updating records. However, at the end of the fiscal year ICE locks the data for year-end reporting purposes. Therefore, historically reported removals and returns are based on locked data.
However, if the removals and returns are re-run today for a previous fiscal year, the data will reflect any changes made to the records since the historically reported data was gathered. For example, FY2010 removals run at the end of FY 2010 may be significantly different
from a similar report run in following years. These changes are due to administrative updates that take place as new information becomes available. Actual physical departures which occurred in FY2010 but whose records were not closed out in the database by October 5th
are not accounted for in the FY2010 historical figures. Re-pulling this information today, will reflect any additional records that were closed out from October 5th until today who physically departed in FY2010.
