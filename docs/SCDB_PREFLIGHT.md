## 4. How versions are published

Annual, late summer: `_brickFiles/<YYYY_NN>/SCDB_<YYYY_NN>_caseCentered_Citation.csv.zip`. Both candidate follow-ons (`2026_02`, `2027_01`) returned 404 today. The freshness workflow HEADs the next release of the same year and release 01 of the next, monthly; host trouble warns and never fails.

## 5. Term labelling

SCDB `term` 2025 = October 2025 to June 2026. Display is the bare year ("2025") with the footnote that a term runs October to June, matching the Court explorer.
