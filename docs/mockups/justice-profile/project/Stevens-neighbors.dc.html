John Paul Stevens

Appointed by Gerald Ford (R)Served 1975–2010 · 35 termsConfirmed 98–0
Biography

Born in Chicago in 1920, Stevens practiced antitrust law and sat on the Seventh Circuit before President Ford nominated him in 1975; the Senate confirmed him 98–0. A lifelong registered Republican, he ended his tenure on the Court’s liberal side. He retired in 2010 and died in 2019.

About these scores

Martin–Quinn scores place each justice on a single liberal–conservative dimension, re-estimated every term from their votes in divided cases. Higher is more conservative. The shaded band is the credible interval: wide in a justice’s first terms, narrower as the record grows.

These scores are not on the same scale as Congress’s DW-NOMINATE and shouldn’t be compared with it directly. Dot color shows the party of the appointing president, not the justice. Source: Martin–Quinn scores (Andrew D. Martin and Kevin M. Quinn), mqscores.lsa.umich.edu.
