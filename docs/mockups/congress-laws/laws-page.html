Mockup — a new “Laws” section under Congress (Congress → Ideology · Laws · Demographics · Wealth), built to sit beside Supreme Court → Decisions: pinned filter bar with the years slider and president band, three linked chart cards, then a scrolling list of every law. Click things — the legend, the Number / Share toggle, the heatmap rows and measure toggle, the party-control checkbox, the list filters, “Major laws”.
  • Illustrative numbers. The pipeline is not built. Per-Congress totals are real for the 93rd (651), 85th and 118th (274), which I read from the Congress.gov API; the rest are approximate (from memory, within a few of the official counts). The policy-area mix, vote bands and “major law” counts are placeholders shaped to look plausible. Do not read any trend off them. The first pre-flight job is replacing them with the real thing.
  • List rows are real laws (names, Pub. L. numbers, dates, sponsors, tallies from my memory, to be re-checked in the pipeline). Policy-area labels and one-line summaries are illustrative; the real summary is the first sentence of the CRS summary. The last row is a placeholder for a law passed with no recorded vote.
  • The year slider is drawn, not wired. The filter bar, cards and list use the same chrome and rules as the Decisions page (rules 3–5b, 7, 10a–10h, 15).
